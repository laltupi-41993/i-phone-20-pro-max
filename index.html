The Research Computing Centre (RCC) provides coordinated management and support of The University of Queensland’s sustained and substantial investment in digital research infrastructure.

RCC helps UQ researchers across disciplines make the most of the University’s eResearch technologies, such as high-performance computing (HPC), research data storage, visualisation and workflows.  

Learn more about the centre

  • High-performance computing
    UQ researchers have access to a range of HPC resources, both locally and nationally.
  • Cloud computing
    RCC works with partners to provide simple and effective cloud computing solutions.
  • Software Platforms & Tools
    Platforms and workflow tools to coordinate multiple resources and bridge hardware and software gaps. 
  • Data management
    RCC can provide advice, tools and other technologies to help UQ researchers manage their data well.
  • Expertise
    RCC has expertise in HPCs, machine learning, data visualisation, web portals, digital HASS, and more.
  • Data Storage
    RCC can help with data archiving, preservation, management and workflows.
